Why we started cooking for others

It began with meals packed for neighbours who missed home food while working away from their families. Word travelled faster than we expected, and within a year the kitchen table wasn't big enough for the orders.

6years open
40+dishes on rotation
3generations of recipes

Every plate follows the same rule as it did at home: nothing cooked in large batches so it never sits, vegetables bought each morning from the same two farmers, and a specials list that changes with the season.


We still don't have a menu card printed — it changes too often for that. What you'll find below is what's usually on offer, but ask us what's fresh today.
